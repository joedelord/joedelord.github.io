About Me

I first studied IT back in 2002, but my path into software really started in 2017, when I started teaching myself web development through my own projects. In 2020 I moved into formal studies at Haaga-Helia, Metropolia and Omnia, followed by an internship as a full-stack developer at Jippo f-Commerce, where I built user-facing and authentication-protected admin interfaces.

From 2022 to 2026 I ran Plantor Oy together with a business partner. In our two-person company I was responsible for all development, from requirements to delivery: I built PHP/MySQL feedback forms and login systems for client companies, and turned my partner's designs into our own website, including a customer-facing forest growth tracking service and an admin panel for orders and their tracking information. Alongside development, I shared customer communication and business administration.

Since then I've focused on modern full-stack development with React, Node.js, Django and PostgreSQL, with authentication, validation and testing built in from the start rather than added later.

Between my IT studies and software development, I spent ten years in logistics. That taught me to work accurately under pressure and to sort out problems on the spot, which turns out to be useful in debugging too.
